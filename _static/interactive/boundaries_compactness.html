That is compactness — and its catch. A snaking shape with no natural reason for its wanderings is the classic fingerprint of gerrymandering, so optimising for compactness steers the map away from manipulation. But a modest score is not proof of foul play: a constituency that faithfully follows a jagged coastline or an awkward county line will score lower for entirely honest reasons. That is exactly why the Commission weighs compactness against the other criteria instead of chasing the roundest possible shapes.
